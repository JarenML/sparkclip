# SparkClip app icon

The app icon is a blue play shape with a four-point spark cut out of it as negative space, on a charcoal rounded tile. The mark alone (no tile) is used beside the wordmark and in the collapsed sidebar.

## Source and exports

- **Icon master:** `resources/sparkclip-icon.svg`, a 1024 × 1024 vector. The renderer uses it directly as the app-icon tile.
- **Mark:** `resources/sparkclip-mark.svg`, the emblem path from the icon master, cropped to its bounds.
- **Lockups:** `resources/sparkclip-logo.svg` (dark backgrounds) and `resources/sparkclip-logo-light.svg` (light backgrounds), generated by `scripts/icon/build-logo.py` from the mark and the Geist wordmark.
- **App assets:** `build/icon.png` (1024px), `build/icon.icns` (macOS) and `build/icon.ico` (Windows: 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 128, 256px).
- **Preview:** open `scripts/icon/icon.html` to inspect 16–128px sizes against dark and light surfaces.

Run `npm run icons` to render the app assets from the vector master. It works on Windows, macOS and Linux, and renders every size from the vector, so small sizes stay crisp. If you change the mark, rerun `python3 scripts/icon/build-logo.py` (needs `pip install fonttools brotli uharfbuzz`). Edit the sources, not the exports.

## Generation prompt

```text
Design a premium, enterprise-grade desktop app icon for "SparkClip", a professional AI tool that turns long-form video into short-form clips.

CONCEPT
A refined abstract monogram built from negative space: a solid rounded-square play-button shape in which a precise four-point spark is cut out as negative space, positioned slightly off-center toward the upper right, as if light is breaking through the frame. The play direction should be implied subtly by the overall shape leaning right, not by a literal cartoon play button. The mark should feel intelligent, calm and precise: the visual language of a serious creative-software company, not a social media sticker.

COMPOSITION
- Square 1024x1024 canvas with a fully transparent background outside the tile.
- A rounded-square app tile, centered, about 82% of the canvas (about 9% padding on each side), with smooth continuous (squircle) corners.
- The tile has a very subtle, almost imperceptible vertical gradient from #16181F at the top to #0E0F13 at the bottom, with a hairline 1px inner border in white at 6% opacity for depth.
- The emblem is optically centered and takes about 56% of the tile width.

COLORS
- Emblem: a single solid color, refined electric blue #5C8FFF.
- The spark cut-out reveals the dark tile beneath it.
- No other colors.

STYLE
Swiss-inspired minimalism, precise geometry built on a consistent grid, balanced proportions, perfectly consistent corner radii, crisp antialiased edges. Timeless and understated, in the same quality tier as the app icons of Linear, Figma, Notion, Arc Browser or Raycast. Must remain clean and recognizable at 16x16 pixels.

DO NOT INCLUDE
Text, letters, multiple colors, neon glow, lens flares, sparkles or confetti, fire, lightning bolts, scissors, film strips, perforations, cameras, brains, circuit patterns, 3D rendering, heavy gradients, glossy reflections, bevels, textures, outer drop shadows, a checkerboard drawn as the background, device mockups, or multiple variations. Produce exactly one icon.
```
